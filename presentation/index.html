Steam Review Filter & Score

Cutting the junk out of Steam reviews, so what's left actually tells you what people think of the game.

The Problem

Not every review is worth counting

A one-word "good game" counts exactly the same as a real, thought-out review. That waters down the score.

Defining "Low Effort"

What actually makes a review junk?

No one hand-labels this, so we built a simple rule from the data itself:

✂️
Too short
under 20 characters
⏱️
Barely played
under 1 hour
📋
Copy-pasted
duplicate text

Any one of these = flagged. This label only trains the model — the live app judges purely from the words themselves.
